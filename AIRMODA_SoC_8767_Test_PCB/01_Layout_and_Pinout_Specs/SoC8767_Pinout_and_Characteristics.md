Design: SoC8767 (Release v1.0.0)

Technology: IHP SG13G2 130 nm BiCMOS

1. QFN-24 Package Pinout & Die Pad Mapping

Pin numbering runs counter-clockwise starting at Pin 1 (top-left lead near corner dot). Corner leads (1, 6, 7, 12, 13, 18, 19, 24) are NC (electrically floating, unbonded). Exposed Die Attach Paddle (DAP) is connected to VSS (PCB GND).

2. Electrical & System Characteristics (SoC8767 / AIRMODA)

| Parameter | Specification / Value | Notes / Test Conditions |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Process Node | IHP SG13G2 (130 nm BiCMOS) | Open-source PDK |

| External Supply (VDD_3V3) | 1.8 V – 3.3 V (Nominal 3.3 V) | Main input to on-chip PMU |

| Bandgap Reference (BGR_1V0) | 1.0 V nominal | Temperature-compensated reference (-40 °C to 125 °C) |

| Core / Digital Supply (VDD_1V2) | 1.2 V nominal (up to 24 mA load) | Generated by on-chip digital LDO |

| Analog Supply (VDD_1V2 / 1.5V internal) | 1.2 V external pin / 1.5 V internal LDO (up to 32.5 mA) | PSRR > 50 dB |

| PMU Macro Area | 95 µm × 110 µm (0.0105 mm²) | Integrated on-die |

| Digital I/O Logic Level | 1.2 V CMOS | CRITICAL: Do NOT drive RESET, AD0, ENABLE, I_SDA, I_SCL, or CLK_D directly with 3.3 V logic! |

| Communication Interface | Split-pin I2C (I_SDA, O_SDA, I_SCL, O_SCL) | Requires external open-drain / bidirectional level-shifting interface on Test PCB |

| Operating Temperature | -40 °C to +125 °C | Industrial / automotive sensor range |

3. Die & Layout Reference Summary

Die Dimensions & Pad Ring: 16 peripheral bond pads (4 per side), pad opening 55.8 µm, gold ball-wedge wire bonding.

Coordinate Reference (Die Center = 0, 0):

Left side (P1–P4): $X = -407.5,\mu\text{m}$, $Y \in {142.9, 47.9, -47.6, -142.5},\mu\text{m}$

Bottom side (P5–P8): $Y = -407.5,\mu\text{m}$, $X \in {-142.5, -47.6, 47.9, 142.9},\mu\text{m}$

Right side (P9–P12): $X = +407.5,\mu\text{m}$, $Y \in {-142.5, -47.6, 47.9, 142.9},\mu\text{m}$

Top side (P13–P16): $Y = +407.5,\mu\text{m}$, $X \in {142.9, 47.9, -47.6, -142.5},\mu\text{m}$
